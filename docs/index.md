# Local Services Ads Setup Checklist for Law Firms

Google Local Services Ads (LSA) are the "Google Screened" listings that often appear above the regular ads and the map for searches like "divorce lawyer near me". The firm pays per lead, not per click. This checklist walks through setup and the weekly management most firms skip. Published by Legal Web Agency.

Eligibility, screening steps and pricing vary by country, state and practice area and change over time, so confirm the current requirements in your own LSA account.

## Before you apply

| Item | Why |
|------|-----|
| Firm details match the Google Business Profile exactly | Mismatched name, address or phone slows verification |
| Each attorney's bar license details ready | Licenses are checked during screening |
| Malpractice insurance details ready, if your market asks for it | Part of screening in many markets |
| Business hours you can actually staff | Leads go cold fast; ads should only run when someone answers |
| A list of practice areas and job types you want | You can switch off the ones you do not take |

## Profile setup

1. Choose only the practice areas and case types you genuinely want.
2. Set the service area to where clients can realistically reach you.
3. Add a short, accurate business description; the same advertising rules apply as on your website.
4. Upload real photos of the attorneys and office.
5. Connect reviews: the LSA profile shows Google reviews, so a steady review flow matters.

## Budget and bidding

- Start with a weekly budget you are comfortable paying entirely in leads.
- Watch cost per lead and cost per signed case, not just lead volume.
- Pause practice areas that produce leads you never sign.

## Weekly management (15 minutes)

| Task | Why |
|------|-----|
| Listen to or review each lead | Know what you paid for |
| Mark leads as booked, archived or not relevant | The platform uses this feedback |
| Dispute leads that are clearly invalid (spam, wrong practice, outside your area) | Valid disputes can be credited |
| Reply to new reviews | Reviews affect ranking and trust |
| Check that the phone was answered in hours | Missed calls are paid leads lost |

## Common mistakes

- Running ads outside staffed hours.
- Leaving every practice area switched on.
- Never disputing invalid leads.
- Letting the business hours, address or phone drift out of sync with the Business Profile.
